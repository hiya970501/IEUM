Afterglow concept cabin

Base model: Modern Car Interior, Grab3D
https://grab3d.com/models/vehicles/modern-car-interior/
https://grab3d.com/dl/modern-car-interior/modern-car-interior.glb
License: CC0-1.0; see cabin-LICENSE.txt.
This is an unbranded concept interior, not Hyundai IONIQ CAD or vehicle scan data.
Afterglow modifications: charcoal / warm-stone palette and separate directional
setting illustration meshes and animated particles. These are not a CFD solver,
duct velocity measurements, seat temperature measurements or a humidity sensor.
The original mesh, PBR normal and roughness maps are retained.

Rendering: Google Filament 1.75.0 (Apache-2.0).
Bundled indirect studio light: Google Filament helmet/default_env asset.
https://google.github.io/filament/web/assets/helmet/default_env/default_env_ibl.ktx
